Every export and import job on one board, with its current step.

Quotes that compare lines

Client profiles

  • Lanes, commodity, volume, payment terms and target rate

Client portal

Customers see their own shipments, and nothing else.

  • Accept a quote in one click

A quick tour

Screens from the live demo. All names, rates and containers are sample data.

Jobs & deadlines

See what is about to cost money.

The job board shows each shipment’s step, containers and next deadline. The tracker counts down free time and cut-offs and estimates demurrage, so nothing sneaks up on you.

Quotes

From request to quote in minutes.

Paste the customer’s message. ForwardDesk reads it, flags anything missing and lays out 2–3 line options with transit, sailing, free time and validity, so you can choose what goes in the customer quote.

Agent CRM

Your agents, not a shared address book.

Add, edit and archive the agents on your account. Keep contacts with call, email and WhatsApp links, the lanes they cover, and a log of activity. Licence and insurance get expiry alerts. Win rate and reply time are taken only from real quotes and logged activity. When you quote, preferred agents come first and blacklisted ones never appear.

Job documents

Issue the B/L, and hold it until you are paid.

Each job has a documents tab: booking confirmation and a house bill of lading on exports, arrival notice and delivery order on imports. They move from draft to issued to released, and download as PDFs in English or Spanish. The B/L or delivery order cannot be released until every charge is invoiced and paid.

House B/L

A clean house bill of lading from the job.

The house B/L is generated from the job details, marked clearly as a sample in the demo, and issued as a PDF you can send once the charges are settled.

Billing

Buy, sell, profit and the invoice, on the job.

Add charge lines for what you buy and what you sell. See revenue, cost, profit and margin. Raise a client invoice as a PDF, record agent bills and payments, and type in exchange rates yourself — they are never fetched or guessed.

Finance

What is owed, and what is overdue.

The finance page lists amounts to collect, to pay and overdue, with an ageing breakdown and balances by client. Sample amounts in the demo are labelled as illustrative only.

Customer PDF

A clean PDF your customer can read.

Route, options comparison and terms on one page, with sell prices only. Add your company name and logo.

Client portal

Let customers act without the email chain.

Each customer signs in to their own portal to accept quotes and approve draft B/Ls. Your team sees it straight away.

How it works

Three steps your team already does, with less chasing.

1

Paste the request

Drop in the customer’s email or chat message. ForwardDesk reads it and flags what’s missing.

2

Compare and send

Pick from 2–3 line options and send a clean PDF with sell prices only.

3

Track, invoice and release

The job lands on the board with its deadlines counting down. Issue the house B/L, invoice the client, and release documents once every charge is paid. The customer follows along in the portal.
