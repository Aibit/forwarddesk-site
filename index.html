Everything on one desk

Jobs & deadline tracker

Every export and import job on one board, with its current step.

  • Free-time, document cut-off and demurrage countdowns, live
  • Unpaid charges before B/L release and guarantee cheques in view
  • Alerts only where money is actually at risk

Quotes that compare lines

Paste a customer request and get 2–3 line options side by side.

  • Recommended, cheapest, fastest and most free time, clearly badged
  • Export and import modes
  • Customer PDF shows sell prices only; buy and margin stay internal

Client profiles

Everything you need to quote a customer again, in one place.

  • Lanes, commodity, volume, payment terms and target rate
  • HS codes with chapter names
  • MSDS flagged by HS chapter, with uploads per lane

Client portal

Customers see their own shipments, and nothing else.

  • Accept a quote in one click
  • Approve the draft B/L
  • Sell prices only: no buy rates, no margins

A quick tour

Screens from the live demo. All names, rates and containers are sample data.

Jobs & deadlines

See what is about to cost money.

The job board shows each shipment’s step, containers and next deadline. The tracker counts down free time and cut-offs and estimates demurrage, so nothing sneaks up on you.

Quotes

From request to quote in minutes.

Paste the customer’s message. ForwardDesk reads it, flags anything missing and lays out 2–3 line options with transit, sailing, free time and validity, so you can choose what goes in the customer quote.

Customer PDF

A clean PDF your customer can read.

Route, options comparison and terms on one page, with sell prices only. Add your company name and logo.

Client portal

Let customers act without the email chain.

Each customer signs in to their own portal to accept quotes and approve draft B/Ls. Your team sees it straight away.

How it works

Three steps your team already does, with less chasing.

1

Paste the request

Drop in the customer’s email or chat message. ForwardDesk reads it and flags what’s missing.

2

Compare and send

Pick from 2–3 line options and send a clean PDF with sell prices only.

3

Track to delivery

The job lands on the board with its deadlines counting down, and the customer follows along in the portal.
